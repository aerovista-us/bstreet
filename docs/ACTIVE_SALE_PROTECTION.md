# 1007 Active-Sale Protection Notes

1007 is actively marketed. The working agreement therefore needs to protect the current “yes” long enough for you to complete the survey/title/City diligence without pretending that 1007 must stop selling the house.

## Core active-sale terms to preserve

- 1007 may continue marketing the property.
- The agreement identifies whether 1007 is actively listed, under contract, not listed, or other.
- 1007 authorizes limited coordination with the listing broker, surveyor and title/escrow company.
- If 1007 accepts an offer before the BLA records, you should receive prompt notice, ideally within one business day.
- Before that sale closes, the parties use commercially reasonable efforts either to finish the BLA first or obtain written cooperation/assumption from the incoming buyer.
- No one tells a prospective buyer that the BLA is already approved if it is not.
- No one promises that 1011 will later receive two legal lots.

## Buyer-assumption concept

If an incoming buyer will own 1007 before the BLA finishes, the buyer-assumption document should confirm that:

- buyer has received the pending BLA/gift materials;
- buyer understands the small front-only concept;
- buyer agrees to continue reasonable cooperation after closing, subject to the same survey, City, title, lender and no-harm conditions;
- buyer is not required to transfer a materially different area without separate written approval;
- buyer may use independent counsel/title/survey/lender advice;
- no final land transfer occurs until the survey-backed warranty deed and Record of Survey are approved and recorded.

## Working diligence timing

The current planning structure is intentionally short because 1007 is on the market:

- initial diligence push: **15 business days**;
- limited extension if survey/City/title/lender work is actively pending outside your control;
- working long-stop: about **45 calendar days**, unless extended in writing.

Idaho counsel/title should finalize the enforceable timing language before the parties rely on it as a binding real-property contract.

## Optional reliance-cost clause

If you spend money after 1007 signs, you may choose between two approaches:

### Neighbor-friendly
Each side bears its own costs if the transaction later stops.

### Protect your reliance
If 1007 voluntarily kills the process solely for convenience or closes a sale without BLA completion / buyer assumption, despite no survey/City/title/lender/no-harm failure, 1007 reimburses documented nonrefundable third-party diligence costs up to a negotiated cap.

This protection should not apply when the deal fails because the geometry, City, title, lender or no-harm conditions fail.

## Listing broker notice

The broker notice should say, in plain language:

- a minor boundary adjustment is being investigated;
- the concept involves approximately the front one foot × 30 feet of the shared line;
- the line is intended to return to the existing boundary before the garage area;
- the BLA has not yet been approved or recorded;
- any buyer should be told the pending BLA may need to be completed before closing or assumed/cooperated with in writing;
- the broker is acknowledging receipt only and is not becoming a party or guarantor.

## Final recording rule

The same-day agreement is not the final conveyance. Do not record a conceptual ~30-sf standalone sliver. The actual transfer occurs only through the approved BLA, survey-backed legal description, warranty deed and Record of Survey coordinated by the surveyor/title company/City.
