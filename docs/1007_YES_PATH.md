# 1007 Says Yes — Your Immediate Action Path

This is written directly to the **1011 homeowner**. AeroVista is your research consultant. You are the person who decides, hires professionals, approves spending, and signs your documents.

## Why you act immediately

1007 is actively marketed for sale. A friendly verbal yes can become much harder to use after a buyer is under contract. Your goal is to capture the current owner’s consent in writing and start the diligence work immediately.

**YES → SIGN → OPTIONAL NOTARY → LISTING NOTICE → RUSH SURVEY/TITLE → CITY/BLA → FINAL DEED**

## 1. Confirm the yes

Say:

> “Just so we’re clear, you’re willing to gift the small front piece if the survey and City show it doesn’t create a problem for your property?”

You want an unambiguous answer.

## 2. Sign the same-day cooperation agreement

Complete the working **Conditional Boundary Gift & Active-Sale Cooperation Agreement** with:

- full owner names;
- date and time;
- both parent parcel addresses/APNs;
- 1007’s current listing status;
- the conceptual front-only area;
- active-sale section initials;
- signatures.

This captures present intent and cooperation. It is **not the final deed**.

## 3. Sign survey access

1007 should authorize reasonable exterior access for the licensed surveyor to locate:

- common line and corners;
- garage / structure walls and eaves near the line;
- fences and driveways;
- visible utilities/service features;
- other items needed for the BLA.

## 4. Use a notary if available

If a notary is available the same day, use the optional acknowledgment. Bring government photo ID.

The notary helps prove who signed and when. It does not substitute for the later survey-backed deed.

## 5. Scan everything before leaving

- scan/photograph every signed page;
- verify signatures and initials;
- give 1007 a complete copy;
- keep the original safe;
- save a cloud copy.

## 6. Notify the listing agent the same day

The property can remain for sale. The goal is simply to prevent an incoming buyer, broker, lender or title company from being surprised by the pending BLA.

## 7. Order rush survey and title immediately

Do this the same day or next business morning.

Do not wait because the owners are friends. An accepted offer can change the transaction quickly.

## 8. If 1007 accepts an offer before the BLA records

Coordinate immediately with the seller, listing agent and title company. The preferred alternatives are:

1. complete the approved BLA before the 1007 sale closes; or
2. have the incoming buyer agree in writing to continue reasonable cooperation after closing, subject to the buyer’s lender/title/legal review.

## Critical rule: do not sign a blank deed

The final Gift Warranty Deed waits for:

- licensed survey;
- final metes-and-bounds description;
- City BLA approval;
- acceptable title;
- any required lender/partial release;
- title/Idaho counsel review;
- correct recording sequence.

The conceptual “1 ft × ~30 ft” description is not the final legal description.

## Same-day checklist

- [ ] Clear yes from 1007.
- [ ] All known owners/co-owners identified.
- [ ] Agreement completed with date/time/listing status.
- [ ] Active-sale section initialed.
- [ ] Survey access signed.
- [ ] Agreement signed.
- [ ] Optional notary completed if available.
- [ ] Complete copies delivered to both sides.
- [ ] Listing agent notice sent.
- [ ] Survey quote/order sent.
- [ ] Title order opened for both parcels.
- [ ] No blank or guessed deed signed.
