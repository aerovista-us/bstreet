# Paid Fallback — If 1007 Declines the Gift

Use this only after the gift is declined. You are the 1011 homeowner. AeroVista is your research consultant.

## Rules

- Do not disclose your mortgage pressure, debt balance, urgency, or internal value estimates.
- Be truthful that the adjustment may improve frontage and future options, but subdivision is not guaranteed.
- Let 1007 name a number first whenever possible.
- Never bid against yourself.
- Decide your same-call authority before the conversation.

Earlier planning references were about **$2,500** as a first cash anchor if you are forced to make one, and about **$5,000** as a same-call ceiling only if you explicitly pre-authorize it. These are negotiating references, not appraisals.

## Transition after “no”

> “I understand. If a gift doesn’t work for you, is there a number that would make the small front piece worth doing, assuming the survey and City confirm it doesn’t hurt your property?”

Then stop talking and let them answer.

## If they say “you make me an offer”

If you pre-authorized the $2,500 reference:

> “I was hoping to keep this simple because it’s such a small piece and I’d be paying the survey, title and City costs. I could consider $2,500, subject to the survey, City, title and lender pieces all working.”

If you did not pre-authorize a number, do not invent one live.

## If they say $5,000

Only agree if $5,000 is inside your pre-authorized authority:

> “If the survey and City confirm the front-only adjustment works and doesn’t create a problem for your place, I can work with $5,000. We’d still have title and any lender approval to clear, and we’d put the paid version in writing.”

## If they say $10,000, $15,000, or anything above your ceiling

> “That’s more than I’m prepared to agree to on the spot. Let me look at the total survey, City and title cost and I’ll get back to you.”

Write the number down. Do not counter upward immediately.

## If they ask what it is really worth to you

> “We don’t know yet. We still need a licensed survey and a City answer, so I’m not treating subdivision or a particular value increase as guaranteed.”

## If they worry about the garage or 1007 value

> “That’s why I only want the front portion and why the agreement stops if the survey shows the line creates a problem for your garage, access, utilities or property.”

## If they refuse outright

> “Understood. I appreciate you hearing me out. I’m not going to pressure you. If you change your mind before the property sells, let me know.”

## Authority card

| Decision | Rule |
|---|---|
| Gift | First ask. |
| First cash anchor if forced | About $2,500 only if pre-authorized. |
| Same-call maximum | About $5,000 only if pre-authorized. |
| Above your ceiling | Stop and re-run the economics. |
| Money agreed | Convert the transaction to a paid BLA agreement through title/Idaho counsel. |

## Important documentation rule

The moment money or other consideration for the land is agreed, the operative transaction is no longer a pure gift. Do not simply write a price onto the gift deed. Have title/Idaho counsel document the paid BLA conveyance and the actual consideration.

## After-call record

Write down the date/time, who participated, gift response, price stated by 1007, your response, whether you stayed inside your authority, and the next promised action/date.
