# B Street — 1011 N B St Homeowner Exit Playbook

AeroVista research workspace for the owner of **1011 N B St, Coeur d’Alene, Idaho (APN C3510003001A)**.

## Purpose

This repository is the source-controlled homeowner decision guide. The strategy is to prove the high-value frontage/subdivision opportunity with the least possible owner cash, then choose the exit that best eliminates the mortgage while preserving equity.

Current preferred path, if survey / City / title / lender work is green:

**prove the split → sell the house-side parcel → pay off the mortgage → retain the buildable lot**.

## Important role distinction

AeroVista is a **research and strategy consultant** providing decision support, research organization, scripts, checklists and working-draft documents. AeroVista is not the homeowner's attorney, broker, lender, surveyor, title company, CPA, appraiser or City representative and does not bind either property owner.

The **1011 homeowner is the decision-maker and contracting party**.

## Core concept

Public-record reconstruction strongly suggests 1011 is approximately **99 ft × 117.5 ft** because a south one-foot portion of original Lot 2 is now associated with 1007. The preferred concept is not to reacquire the full-depth strip; it is to test a **front-only ~1 ft × 30 ft boundary line adjustment**, returning to the existing common line before the 1007 garage area.

If a licensed survey and the City recognize approximately **100.00 ft of B Street frontage**, the owner may have a credible path toward two future 50-foot-frontage R-12 lots. That remains a hypothesis until the survey, City, title and lender gates are satisfied.

## Professional gates

Do not rely on a future two-lot sale until a licensed survey, title review and written City direction support the geometry. Use Idaho counsel/title to finalize binding neighbor documents and the deed; use the mortgage servicer for release/recast/payoff rules; use a CPA for tax sequencing.
