Same-day action

1007 said “yes.” What you do before you leave.

1
Confirm the yes.
“Just so we're clear, you're willing to gift the small front piece if the survey and City show it doesn't create a problem for your property?”
2
Sign the Conditional Boundary Gift & Active-Sale Cooperation Agreement.
Fill names, date/time and listing status. Initial the active-sale section. This captures the present agreement; it is not the deed.
3
Sign survey access.
Allow a licensed surveyor reasonable exterior access to locate the common line, garage/walls, corners and visible utilities.
4
Use a notary if available.
Bring government ID. Notarizing the cooperation agreement helps prove who signed and when; it does not transfer unsurveyed land.
5
Scan every signed page and give both sides copies.
6
Notify 1007's listing agent the same day.
The property can remain for sale. The point is to keep an incoming buyer/title company from being surprised.
7
Order rush survey and title work.
Do it the same day or next business morning.
8
If 1007 accepts an offer first, coordinate immediately.
Either finish the BLA before 1007 closes or have the incoming buyer sign a buyer-assumption/cooperation document, subject to buyer/lender/title review.
Do not pre-sign a blank deed. The actual warranty deed waits for the licensed surveyor's legal description, City/title path and any required lender release.
First-time owner guide

What happens after that

  1. Survey: exact B Street frontage, common boundary, 1007 garage/walls, your house footprint, easements/access/utilities, proposed front-only BLA, conceptual future 50/50 split.
  2. Title on both parcels: current owners/vesting, legal descriptions, deeds of trust, liens, easements and whether 1007's lender must partially release the transfer area.
  3. City written questions: will the front-only jog create recognized 100.00-foot frontage, and can the future southern lot have 50 feet at B Street but narrow to roughly 49 feet farther back?
  4. Only if green: finalize BLA documents through Idaho counsel/title, submit the City BLA, and let the surveyor prepare the Record of Survey and final deed exhibit.
  5. Re-price before going farther: get BPOs for whole today, whole with proven feasibility, house-side future parcel and vacant future parcel.
  6. Mortgage: get written payoff, partial-release and recast rules. Do not assume a vacant-lot sale lowers the scheduled payment.
  7. Choose the least expensive exit that actually solves your mortgage problem. You do not need to finish every possible entitlement step.

Preferred money path

Why house-side first still leads

If a separately transferable house-side parcel can sell for enough to pay the roughly $300k–$400k mortgage in full, escrow can retire the loan and coordinate release/reconveyance while you keep the buildable lot. Selling the vacant half first can instead require a lender partial release and heavy principal curtailment without automatically lowering your monthly payment unless the lender permits a recast/refinance.

What your same-day contract is designed to capture

  • Present gift intent; no purchase price unless later amended in writing.
  • Concept area only—licensed survey controls the final legal description.
  • Immediate survey/title/City cooperation.
  • Active-listing notice and prompt notice if 1007 accepts an offer.
  • Buyer-assumption route if a sale happens before BLA recording.
  • Initial 15-business-day diligence push and a limited long-stop structure.
  • No final deed until survey, City, title, no-harm and lender conditions are satisfied.
  • No recording of a stand-alone tiny sliver; the transfer merges into 1011 through the BLA.
  • No promise that CDA will later approve two lots.
